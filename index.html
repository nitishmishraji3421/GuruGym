10 / FAQ

QUESTIONS?
WE GOT YOU.

Everything you need before you decide to visit, enquire or start training.

Where is Guru Gym located?+

3rd Floor, Thakur Bari Rd, SNP Area, Ambagan, Sakchi, Jamshedpur, Jharkhand 831001.

What membership plans are available?+

Starter (1 month), Standard (3 months), Premium (6 months), Couple Special (3 months) and Ultimate (12 months).

How do I enquire about membership?+

Use the membership buttons on this page or message the gym directly on WhatsApp. Your selected plan can be pre-filled for you.

Can I see the gym before joining?+

Yes — use the visit/contact section to call the gym team or open the location in Google Maps.

What should I bring?+

Your own towel and water bottle, plus a willingness to train consistently and follow the gym's safety guidelines.
